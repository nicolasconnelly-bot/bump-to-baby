W-16 · About 16 weeks before your due date — roughly week 24 of a 40-week pregnancy.

Clothing, Round One

Don't buy baby clothes yet (seriously)

Buy now

2–3 zip sleepers (0–3 mo).

Can wait

Everything else.

Baby clothes are the most-gifted category by far. Hold off until after your shower. The one exception is a few 0–3 month zip-up sleepers in your favorite style, because zippers beat snaps at 3 AM.

Check your quiz list for the quantities for your season. Leave newborn-size clothes to gifts, since some babies skip that size entirely.
