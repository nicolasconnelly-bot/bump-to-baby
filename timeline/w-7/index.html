W-7 · About 7 weeks before your due date — roughly week 33 of a 40-week pregnancy.

Wash Everything

Laundry day (the cutest one you'll ever have)

Buy now

Gentle detergent, extra burp cloths.

Can wait

Bigger sizes.

Wash baby clothes, sheets, swaddles and burp cloths in a gentle, fragrance-free detergent. Then fold everything by size. A bin per size (NB, 0–3, 3–6) makes life easier later.

Make up the bassinet and crib with fitted sheets and mattress protectors. Stock 6–10 burp cloths around the house.
