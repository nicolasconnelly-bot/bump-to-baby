Plan the Room (or the Corner)

Nursery planning: start with a tape measure

Buy now

Paint, if you're painting (let it air out for weeks).

Can wait

Furniture.

Measure the space where baby will sleep. Safe sleep guidance recommends room-sharing for the first months, so many babies start in a bassinet next to your bed even when there's a nursery ready.

Sketch where the crib, dresser and chair go, keeping the crib away from windows, cords and shelves. Skip bumpers, pillows and quilts in the crib. They look nice but aren't safe.
