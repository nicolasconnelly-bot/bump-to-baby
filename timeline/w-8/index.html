W-8 · About 8 weeks before your due date — roughly week 32 of a 40-week pregnancy.

Health Kit

Build the 2 AM medicine cabinet

Buy now

Thermometer, aspirator, saline.

Can wait

Fancy humidifiers (unless it's winter).

Put together a small kit: a digital thermometer (rectal readings are most accurate for newborns), a nasal aspirator, saline drops, and infant acetaminophen (ask your pediatrician about dosing before you ever use it).

Write down the pediatrician's after-hours number. Check your smoke and CO detectors while you're at it.
