Nice-to-haves.

Your quiz results are your registry starter kit. Add the must-haves first, then a few nice-to-haves at different prices so every guest has options.

Most stores give completion discounts, often around 10–15%, on whatever's left near your due date, so it's fine to register for big items you plan to buy yourself. Skip anything with "warmer," "organizer" or "system" in the name until you know you need it.
