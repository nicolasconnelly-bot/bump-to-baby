W-4 · About 4 weeks before your due date — roughly week 36 of a 40-week pregnancy.

Set Up Stations

Feeding station + changing station = calmer nights

Buy now

A dim night light.

Can wait

Gliders and recliners, if you have a comfy chair.

Set up a nighttime feeding spot: a chair, water bottle, snacks, burp cloth, phone charger, and a dim light. Do the same for each changing station.

These small setups save a lot of trips back and forth. Practice swaddling a stuffed animal. It sounds silly, but it works.
