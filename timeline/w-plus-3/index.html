Sizing Up

Newborn clothes already snug? Here's what to do

Buy now

Next diaper size, a carrier if you need one.

Can wait

6+ month clothes.

Some babies outgrow newborn size within weeks. Check the snaps and the diaper fit. Red marks on the legs mean it's time to size up.

Move to size 1 diapers and 0–3 month clothes, and pack away the outgrown stuff to hand down or resell. Now's a good time to look at a carrier for hands-free walks.
