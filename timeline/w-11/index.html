W-11 · About 11 weeks before your due date — roughly week 29 of a 40-week pregnancy.

Feeding Gear

Feeding gear: start small

Buy now

Pump and essentials, or 2–4 bottles.

Can wait

Sterilizers and formula makers.

Breastfeeding? Order your insurance pump now, plus nipple cream, breast pads, a couple of nursing bras and milk storage bags. Formula? Buy one small can and a few bottles. Babies can be picky about both. Combo? A few of each.

Grab a bottle brush and drying rack either way. Don't stock up on one brand of bottle until baby has tried it.
