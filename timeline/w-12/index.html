Buy and Install the Car Seat

Car seat install weekend

Buy now

Car seat (+ stroller if it's a travel system).

Can wait

Car mirror and seat organizers.

Buy the car seat now and install it, then have it checked. Many fire stations, hospitals and certified child passenger safety technicians (CPSTs) do free checks.

Learn the "inch test" (it shouldn't move more than an inch at the belt path) and check the harness height. Register the seat with the manufacturer so you get recall notices.
