Privacy

Privacy

Updated 2026-10-14. Bump to Baby is a static website. It has no accounts.

What stays in your browser

The registry quiz runs on your device. Answers are put in the page address when you see results, so you can refresh that list. They are not sent to us.

If you enter a due date, the page saves it in this browser under the local storage key btb-due. That lets the timeline highlight your week when you come back. You can clear it by deleting site data for this address in your browser.

Email

The newsletter signup is a MailerLite embedded form named Bump to Baby Signup. It asks for an email address and a due date. Submitting it sends those two fields to MailerLite, which runs the mailing list. MailerLite's privacy policy applies to that submission. Pages that show the form also load MailerLite's script from assets.mailerlite.com.

A due date entered on the timeline stays in this browser. That saved date is not sent to MailerLite unless you also submit the newsletter form.

Affiliate links

Some gear links go to Amazon and include an Associates tag. Amazon handles those visits under its own privacy policy. This site does not receive your Amazon account details. See the affiliate disclosure.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the files and may keep ordinary server logs. This site does not add tracking pixels or advertising cookies of its own.

Children

The site is written for expecting parents. It is not directed at children, and it does not ask children to create accounts.

Contact

Questions about this site can go to the GitHub repository.

About this site

Bump to Baby is a free planning guide for expecting parents. It runs as a static site: the quiz stays in your browser, and the week-by-week notes are ordinary web pages.

General information only. Safe-sleep, car-seat, and medicine notes are not personal medical advice. Read the disclaimer.
