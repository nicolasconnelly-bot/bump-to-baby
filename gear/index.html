Nursery

Safe sleep starts with a firm, empty sleep space. Anchor any dresser to the wall. Skip bumpers, pillows, and quilts in the crib.

Clothing

Use the low end of each range if you do laundry every 2–3 days, and the high end if you'd rather do it less. Zip-up sleepers with two-way zippers beat snaps at 3 AM. Skip baby shoes before walking, fancy outfits, and newborn-size jeans.
